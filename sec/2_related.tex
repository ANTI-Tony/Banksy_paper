\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Scene text editing in images.} Early editors decompose a text image into background, foreground style and content and re-synthesise it with GANs~\cite{wu2019srnet,roy2020stefann,krishnan2023textstylebrush,qu2023mostel}; STEFANN~\cite{roy2020stefann} generates missing characters in the font of an observed one. Diffusion models brought text rendering and editing to arbitrary images through glyph conditioning~\cite{chen2023textdiffuser,yang2023glyphcontrol,tuo2024anytext,tuo2024anytext2,ji2023diffste,zhao2024udifftext,liu2024glyphbyt5,zeng2024textctrl,glyphmastero2025}. These methods produce natural glyphs, but spelling remains a sampling outcome rather than a guarantee, and in video, frame-wise application produces flicker. A related line renders text first and harmonises it afterwards~\cite{zhan2019sfgan,glyphbanana2026}; our learned refiner (\cref{sec:exp_refiner}) belongs to this family.

\paragraph{Text in generated and edited videos.} Text-to-video models~\cite{wan2025wan,yang2025cogvideox,jiang2025vace} render legible text only occasionally: dedicated benchmarks find most open and commercial models unable to produce legible, temporally consistent text~\cite{t2vtextbench2025,vtrbench2026,textvbench2026,avgenbench2026}, and image and video tokenizers already lose small text in reconstruction~\cite{tokbench2025}. Generation-time remedies condition on glyphs~\cite{liu2024textanimator} or fine-tune on synthetic text-rich clips~\cite{vtp2025}; they lower the rate of garbled text but do not repair a given clip. For editing, \steer~\cite{zeng2026steervte} injects line- and character-level glyph features and a VLM style embedding into a frozen VACE backbone and trains on a million synthetic and real triplets. VidScribe~\cite{vidscribe2026} evaluates text generation and localised editing in videos, including commercial models, and finds editing the weakest regime, with edits often corrupting text that should not change; ViTeX-Bench~\cite{vitexbench2026} evaluates video scene-text editing, including edit locality, and releases a 14B reference editor. A concurrent editor~\cite{zhang2026trajglyph} warps rendered target glyphs into the text box of every frame as a per-frame reference for a video diffusion model and supervises it with a frozen recognizer, and releases VTEdit, 288 real clips for text replacement and addition; like \steer it regenerates the text region, whereas we place rendered glyphs deterministically on the tracked carrier and gate every edit on evidence. All of these regenerate the text region. We instead repair generated text with a training-free pipeline that leaves already-correct text untouched, writes the target deterministically and changes only the text region, and we compare against \steer as the strongest generative editor.

\paragraph{Text effects transfer.} Statistics-based and example-based methods transfer the appearance of stylised text to new glyphs~\cite{yang2017awesometypography,men2019dyntypo,yang2019tetgan}, using distance to the glyph boundary as the key coordinate. SAT follows this idea but fits a light-transport operator on the old glyphs of the same video and gates its use on measured reconstruction gain.

\paragraph{Video inpainting and consistent editing.} Removing the old text is a video inpainting problem~\cite{zhou2023propainter,bian2025videopainter}; we inpaint once on a reference frame with LaMa~\cite{suvorov2022lama} and propagate the result along the tracked carrier, which avoids per-frame hallucination. Flow- and correspondence-guided attention improves the temporal consistency of diffusion-based video editing~\cite{cong2024flatten,geyer2024tokenflow}; we test a carrier-anchored attention bias of this kind on our learned refiner (\cref{sec:exp_refiner}).

\paragraph{Evaluating text.} OCR systems~\cite{baek2019craft,shi2017crnn,du2020ppocr} and, increasingly, vision-language models~\cite{bai2025qwen25vl} are used to score generated text. We use a VLM reader for evaluation and a separate OCR inside the pipeline, report repairs and breakages separately, and complement automatic scores with blind human studies.
