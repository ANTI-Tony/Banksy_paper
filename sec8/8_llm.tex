\section*{LLM usage statement}
Large language models were used as assistants for writing and editing the text of this paper, for drafting experiment scripts, and for summarising evaluation outputs. The task definition, the method, the pre-registered criteria, the experiments and their interpretation are the authors' own; every number in the paper is generated from the evaluation files of the stated runs, and the authors verified the claims against those files. Vision-language models are also used as components of the evaluation (readers) and of the verified-selection system, as described in the paper.
