\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Scene text editing.} Early editors decompose a text image into background, style and content and re-synthesise it with GANs~\cite{wu2019srnet,roy2020stefann,krishnan2023textstylebrush,qu2023mostel}; diffusion models brought text rendering and editing to arbitrary images through glyph conditioning~\cite{chen2023textdiffuser,yang2023glyphcontrol,tuo2024anytext,tuo2024anytext2,ji2023diffste,zhao2024udifftext,liu2024glyphbyt5,zeng2024textctrl,glyphmastero2025}. Their glyphs are natural, but spelling remains a sampling outcome, and frame-wise application to video flickers. A related line renders text first and harmonises it afterwards~\cite{zhan2019sfgan,glyphbanana2026}; our learned refiner (\cref{sec:exp_neg}) belongs to it.

\paragraph{Text in generated and edited videos.} Text-to-video models render legible text only occasionally~\cite{t2vtextbench2025,vtrbench2026,textvbench2026,avgenbench2026,tokbench2025}; generation-time remedies condition on glyphs~\cite{liu2024textanimator} or fine-tune on text-rich clips~\cite{vtp2025} but do not repair a given clip. For editing, \steer~\cite{zeng2026steervte} injects glyph features and a VLM style embedding into a frozen VACE backbone trained on a million triplets; VidScribe~\cite{vidscribe2026} and ViTeX-Bench~\cite{vitexbench2026} evaluate video text editing, including commercial models, and find edits that corrupt text that should not change. A concurrent editor~\cite{zhang2026trajglyph} warps rendered target glyphs into the text box of every frame as a per-frame reference for a video diffusion model and supervises it with a frozen recognizer, and releases VTEdit, 288 real clips for text replacement and addition; like \steer it regenerates the text region, whereas we place rendered glyphs deterministically on the tracked carrier and gate every edit on evidence. All of these regenerate the text region. We repair generated text with a training-free pipeline that leaves correct text untouched, writes the target deterministically and changes only the text region, and compare against \steer as the strongest editor.

\paragraph{Text effects, inpainting, evaluation.} Example-based text effects transfer keys appearance on the distance to the glyph boundary~\cite{yang2017awesometypography,men2019dyntypo,yang2019tetgan}; SAT fits such an operator on the old glyphs of the same video and gates its use on measured gain. We remove the old text with LaMa~\cite{suvorov2022lama} once per clip and propagate along the tracked carrier rather than inpainting every frame~\cite{zhou2023propainter,bian2025videopainter}, and we test a carrier-anchored attention bias in the spirit of flow-guided attention~\cite{cong2024flatten,geyer2024tokenflow} on the refiner. Text is scored by OCR~\cite{baek2019craft,shi2017crnn,du2020ppocr} and, increasingly, by vision-language models~\cite{bai2025qwen25vl}; we use a VLM reader for evaluation and a separate OCR inside the pipeline.
